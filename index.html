Trustworthy Scientific AI for Safety-Critical Structures.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at UNIST, advised by Prof. Young-Joo Lee. I build deep learning models that predict how structures — nuclear power plants in particular — respond to earthquakes, and I design them to be interpretable and uncertainty-aware so that engineers can rely on them.

Research연구

Virtual sensing가상 센싱

Virtual sensing from a single seismometer지진계 하나로 하는 가상 센싱

A pretrained deep learning model reconstructs seismic floor responses at 139 locations in a nuclear power plant from a single seismometer, then flags which critical equipment to inspect first after an earthquake.

사전학습된 딥러닝 모델이 지진계 하나의 기록으로 원전 139개 지점의 층 응답을 복원하고, 지진 직후 어떤 핵심 기기부터 점검해야 할지 알려줍니다.

Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering, 2025

Uncertainty불확실성

Uncertainty-aware floor response prediction불확실성을 고려한 층 응답 예측

An attention-enhanced multimodal model fuses ground motion and structural parameters to predict seismic floor responses probabilistically, propagating structural uncertainty through time.

어텐션 기반 멀티모달 모델이 지반운동과 구조 물성을 결합해 층 응답을 확률적으로 예측하고, 구조 불확실성을 시간에 따라 전파합니다.

Reliability Engineering & System Safety, 2026

Interpretability해석 가능성

1D CNN kernels as FIR filtersFIR 필터로 해석하는 1D CNN 커널

Treating 1D CNN kernels as FIR filters links them to the structure's transfer function, which gives a physics-based rule for choosing kernel size and shows which frequency band each kernel captures.

1D CNN 커널을 FIR 필터로 보고 구조물의 전달함수와 연결해, 커널 크기를 물리적으로 정하는 규칙을 얻고 각 커널이 어떤 주파수 대역을 담당하는지 보여줍니다.

Nuclear Engineering and Design, 2026
