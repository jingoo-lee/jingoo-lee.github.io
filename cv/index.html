Professional Summary

Trustworthy Scientific AI for Safety-Critical Structures. (1) Physically interpretable deep learning: 1D CNN kernels (FIR filters) linked to structural transfer functions, yielding a physics-based kernel-size rule K̂ = 1895 / fn (Nuclear Engineering and Design, 2026; SMiRT-28 Heki Shibata Early Career Award, Honorable Mention). (2) Beyond the linear regime: extended the framework to nonlinear hysteretic response under beyond-design-basis shaking — a kinematics-aware loss cut the mean error of a GRU model by 53% (mMAPE 1.00% → 0.47%, Bouc–Wen, at the training intensity), at ~3.4 ms per record on a GPU (≈55–1,100× faster than nonlinear time-history analysis). Operator-learning extensions — a Windowed Recurrent FNO (KNS 2026) and an FNO for eddy-current NDT with CEA List & KRISS — are in preparation. (3) Virtual sensing and uncertainty for NPP seismic safety: floor responses at 139 locations from a single seismometer (1 → 139), probabilistic prediction under structural uncertainty about 105× faster than 100 Monte Carlo FE runs (35 h → <1 s), and inspection prioritization (Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering, 2025; Reliability Engineering & System Safety, 2026).
