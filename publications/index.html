2026

  1. RESS
    Predicting seismic floor response for nuclear power plant structures with time-series uncertainty propagation using attention-enhanced multimodal deep learning
    Jingoo Lee, Seungjun Lee, Young-Joo Lee, and Jaebeom Lee
    Reliability Engineering & System Safety, 2026
    Abs DOI Bib

    An attention-enhanced multimodal deep learning model (Att-MMDL) for probabilistic seismic floor-response prediction that propagates structural uncertainty through the time series.

  2. NED
    Physically interpretable 1D CNN for seismic response prediction: A filter-theoretic approach to kernel design
    Jingoo Lee, Young-Joo Lee, Minsun Kim, and Jaebeom Lee
    Nuclear Engineering and Design, 2026
    Abs DOI Bib

    Establishes an analogy between FIR filters and structural transfer functions to make 1D CNNs physically interpretable and to guide principled kernel-size design for seismic response prediction.

    Virtual sensing of seismic floor responses for rapid prioritization of critical equipment inspection in nuclear power plants
    Jingoo Lee, Seungjun Lee, Young-Joo Lee, and Jaebeom Lee
    Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering, 2025
    Abs DOI Bib

    A deep learning framework that virtually senses seismic floor responses at 139 locations across a nuclear power plant structure from a single seismometer, enabling rapid prioritization of critical equipment for post-earthquake inspection.
