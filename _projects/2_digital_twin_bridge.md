**디지털트윈 및 비전 인공지능 기반 교량 재난대응 상황실 구축**
*Establishment of a bridge disaster-response command center utilizing digital twin and vision-based artificial intelligence.*

- **Funding:** National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF) / Ministry of Science and ICT — 중견연구 (유형 1)
- **Period:** May 2024 – Apr. 2029
- **Role:** Deep learning for structural response prediction and health monitoring of bridges within the digital-twin pipeline.

This mid-career research project builds a digital-twin-based command center for bridge disaster response, fusing physics-based models, sensor data, and vision-based AI. My contribution focuses on data-driven surrogate models and probabilistic structural response prediction that keep the digital twin synchronized with monitoring data for rapid, reliable decision-making.
